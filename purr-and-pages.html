OUR STORY

A little coffee, a lot of stories.

Purr & Pages was imagined as a home away from home for people who love the smell of coffee, the magic of a good book and the quiet company of cats. Here, there is always another story to discover and always room for one more cup.

Tucked into a sunlit neighbourhood corner, our space brings together three of life’s most grounding pleasures: stories that transport you, warm beverages that slow your pulse, and affectionate rescue felines who remind us to savour the present moment.

Whether you are here to lose yourself in an afternoon of undisturbed reading, discover your next literary obsession, or simply let a sleeping cat warm your lap while you sip our house-roasted Chapter One Latte, our doors are open to every curious soul.

Comfort

Plush armchairs, warm amber light, and an unhurried atmosphere.

Curiosity

Forgotten classics, bold independent voices, and inquisitive whiskers.

Community

A safe, gentle gathering place for readers, thinkers, and pet lovers.

A Little Note About Our Little Residents

Our cats are family, not attractions. They are free to rest, wander, or take a break whenever they need. Please let sleeping cats sleep, approach them gently, and follow our team's guidance when interacting with them. Not every cat wants to be petted, and that's perfectly okay.

⚠️
Treat Safety Notice:

Please ask our team before offering any cat treats. Only café-approved treats may be given, in small portions and under staff supervision. Never offer cats coffee, tea, chocolate, cakes, biscuits, dairy drinks, or other food from the human menu.
