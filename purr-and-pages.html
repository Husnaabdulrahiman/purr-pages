Our Little Story

A Place Where Stories Feel Like Home.

Some places are made for rushing through the day. Ours is made for slowing down. At Purr & Pages, stories live beside steaming cups of coffee, sweet treats make every afternoon better, and our curious feline friends remind you to take life one cosy moment at a time.

Whether you are diving into a vintage classic, discovering your next favourite indie author, or simply sharing a quiet moment with a purring companion on your lap, this space was hand-crafted for you.

Treat Time, The Safe Way

Please ask our team before feeding any cat. Only café-approved treats may be given, in small portions and with staff supervision. Some cats have dietary restrictions or may not want treats, and that's perfectly okay. Never feed cats food or drinks from the human menu (no coffee, tea, dairy, chocolate, or pastries).

THE RESIDENTS’ CLUB

The Real Owners of This Place.

Every good bookstore has its characters. Ours just happen to have whiskers.

Every good story has unforgettable characters. At Purr & Pages, ours have whiskers, tiny paws, and a habit of stealing the best reading spots. Meet the little personalities who make our café feel like home.

Sleepy Sweetheart
Mochi 🐾
The Professional Napper
“One more chapter? Make it one more nap.”

Mochi believes that every good book deserves a comfortable reading companion and every afternoon deserves at least three naps...

Social Butterfly
Biscuit ⭐
The Friendly Little Greeter
“You came for coffee. You stayed for me.”

Biscuit considers every visitor a potential best friend. This cheerful little greeter loves inspecting shopping bags and supervising café arrivals...

Curious Chaos
Pepper 📚
The Tiny Mischief-Maker
“I didn't knock it over. The book wanted to explore.”

Pepper is convinced that bookmarks are toys, paper bags are secret hideouts, and every unattended book needs a thorough inspection...

Comfort Seeker
Chai ☕
The Cosy Companion
“Slow sips. Soft paws. Good stories.”

Chai is the living definition of a slow afternoon. This gentle companion enjoys peaceful corners, quiet company, and the sound of pages turning...

Dreamy Reader
Waffles ☁️
The Sleepy Bookworm
“I'll finish this chapter after my nap.”

Waffles starts every day with excellent intentions: explore the café and inspect a few books. Somehow, these plans always end with a nap in the sun...

Little Adventurer
Fig 🧭
The Curious Explorer
“Every corner hides a new chapter.”

Fig is the café's self-appointed explorer. New deliveries, mysterious corners, and freshly arranged bookshelves all demand immediate investigation...

Playful Menace
Pickles 🧶
The Tiny Trouble-Maker
“Trouble? I prefer the term plot development.”

Pickles has two hobbies: making unexpected appearances and acting completely innocent afterward. A rustle of paper sparks instant excitement...

Quiet Observer
Ink ✒️
The Midnight Poet
“Some stories are better told in silence.”

Ink is a quiet soul with a talent for finding the most peaceful corner in the room. Ink prefers to observe from a comfortable distance...

Queen of Comfort
Pudding 👑
The Royal Reviewer
“The cushion is mine. The book may stay.”

Pudding takes comfort very seriously. Every cushion must meet her standards, and she is the café's unofficial quality-control expert...

Curious Kitten
Noodle ✨
The Little Daydreamer
“Life is one big story. Let's explore it.”

Noodle sees magic in the smallest things: a sunbeam on the floor, a ribbon on a gift, or the gentle movement of a page. Curious and full of wonder...

A Little Note About Our Little Residents

Our cats are family, not attractions. They are free to rest, wander, or take a break whenever they need. Please let sleeping cats sleep, approach them gently, and follow our team's guidance when interacting with them. Not every cat wants to be petted, and that's perfectly okay.
